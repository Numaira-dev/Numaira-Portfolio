## Style Guidelines:

- Primary accent color: Deep professional violet (#36248F), signifying creativity and engineering prowess, while maintaining sophistication.
- Background color: Very dark charcoal with a subtle violet tint (#22212C), providing a sleek, modern, and tech-centric dark theme suitable for focused content consumption.
- Secondary accent color: A vibrant blue (#477EEB), providing strong contrast for interactive elements, highlights, and calls to action, drawing attention efficiently.
- Headlines: 'Space Grotesk' (sans-serif), lending a modern, tech-savvy, and clean aesthetic to titles and prominent text.
- Body text: 'Inter' (sans-serif), ensuring excellent readability for longer descriptions, project details, and professional summaries due to its neutral and optimized design.
- Utilize modern, minimalistic, line-based icons for skill categories, contact links, and project functionalities, maintaining a clean and professional look.
- Implement a responsive, grid-based layout for showcasing projects and skills, ensuring optimal viewing experience across all devices. Employ clear section divisions with generous white space for content hierarchy and legibility.
- Incorporate subtle, tasteful micro-animations for navigation interactions, card hovers in the project section, and transitions between sections, enhancing user engagement without being distracting.
